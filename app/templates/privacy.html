Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

1. What Data We Process

{{ app_name }} analyzes the URL you submit to generate a GEO Score. We process: (a) the URL you submit, (b) the public content of that page (HTML, text, metadata), and (c) any question you ask through the "Ask Grezz AI" feature. We don't request or store personal data (name, email, etc) unless it happens to appear on the page you analyzed.

2. Data Storage

Analysis results are cached temporarily on our server (at most a few hours) to speed up repeat scans of the same URL, then automatically deleted. We don't store a permanent history of your analyses unless a future subscription/account feature is introduced.

3. Third Parties

To generate AI-powered summaries and recommendations, the content of the page you analyze is sent to the Google Gemini API for processing. Please review Google's own privacy policy regarding data processing by the Gemini API.

4. Ads & Cookies

We display ads through Google AdSense to help fund this free service. AdSense may use cookies to show more relevant ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalized ads via Google Ads Settings. A cookie consent banner is shown on your first visit.

5. Security

We enforce URL validation to prevent misuse of the system (e.g. targeting internal network addresses), request rate limiting, and standard security headers on all site responses.

6. Your Rights

Since we don't permanently store personal data, there's no account data to delete under free-tier usage. If a subscription/account tier becomes available in the future, you'll be able to request deletion of your account data at any time.

7. Contact

Privacy-related questions can be sent via our Contact page.
