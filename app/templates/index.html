Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between GEO and regular SEO?

SEO optimizes for showing up in a list of search results. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) optimizes for your content being understood and cited directly by AI like ChatGPT, Gemini, or Perplexity when answering a user's question — related goals, but different techniques.

Does Grezz store data about my website?

Analysis results are only cached temporarily on the server to speed up repeat checks, then automatically expire. See full details on our Privacy Policy page.

How long does a scan take?

Usually 10-20 seconds, depending on the page size and how fast the target site responds.

Do Grezz's recommendations guarantee AI will cite my site?

Nothing can guarantee that — AI search algorithms keep evolving. Grezz's recommendations are based on current best-known practices for improving the odds your content gets understood and considered by AI.
